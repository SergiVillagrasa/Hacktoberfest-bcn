# Client briefing side panel

## Mode

Operate. The manager prepares a conversation and reviews recorded context beside
Gmail; the interface must support quick scanning and deliberate evidence checks.

## Reading sequence

Lead with matched identity and partial-history coverage. Open concerns and
commitments receive the first section, as confirmed by Sergi. Goals, preferences,
and message-specific communication signals follow. Expand evidence in place and
keep history/import tools available below the brief.

## Visual and motion guidance

Apply `../../apps/extension/DESIGN.md`. Context-kind icons, review labels, source
quotations, and direction arrows clarify meaning. Brief disclosure/status feedback
supports continuity; nothing blocks reading or implies a risk/confidence score.
Reduced-motion users receive immediate static state changes.
